# Product context

## Why this exists

Traders need one place to log trades with context (screenshots, notes, PnL) and to share technical views with a community, similar to a focused social network around charts.

## Primary users

- Retail traders who journal and optionally share analysis.
- Readers who follow trusted traders for ideas (not financial advice).

## Experience goals

- **Journal**: fast entry of symbol, direction, prices, volume, date; optional screenshot; clear list/sort by `traded_at`.
- **Feed**: chronological posts from followed accounts and own posts; like and expand comments without friction.
- **Profile**: avatar, bio, ability to follow/unfollow.
- **Trust & clarity**: auth errors and validation messages are understandable; uploads show progress and recover from failure.

## Non-goals for UX

- Algorithmic timeline ranking (start with simple chronological + follow filter per spec).
